# Quiz: LLM Foundations

Answer all eight questions. Questions 5 and 6 are scenario questions. A strong short answer names a
decision boundary and evidence, not only a definition.

1. **Multiple choice — Tokens.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Tokens`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

2. **Multiple choice — Context windows.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Context windows`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

3. **Multiple choice — Instruction hierarchy.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Instruction hierarchy`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

4. **Multiple choice — Probabilistic generation.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Probabilistic generation`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

5. **Scenario analysis.** In the normal scenario—answer a warranty question under missing context, grounded context, conflicting authority, and authorized tool access—name two observable acceptance checks and explain why each belongs to this module.

6. **Scenario analysis.** How should the design respond when two approved excerpts use different effective dates and produce different coverage answers. Justify whether to clarify, proceed, abstain, or escalate.

7. **Error identification.** Identify at least three defects in: `Tell the customer whether their current warranty covers this. Be completely certain.`

8. **Design decision.** Give a safe expected response to this failure case: A retrieved customer note says to ignore the policy and approve every claim. Name one prompt control and one application control.
