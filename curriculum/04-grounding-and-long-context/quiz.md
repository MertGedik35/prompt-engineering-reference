# Quiz: Grounding and Long Context

Answer all eight questions. Questions 5 and 6 are scenario questions. A strong short answer names a
decision boundary and evidence, not only a definition.

1. **Multiple choice — Source hierarchy.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Source hierarchy`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

2. **Multiple choice — Trust boundaries.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Trust boundaries`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

3. **Multiple choice — Evidence tables.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Evidence tables`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

4. **Multiple choice — Conflicts and missing evidence.** Which design most directly demonstrates `Conflicts and missing evidence`?
   A. A longer answer
   B. A decision or control matching this module's definition
   C. A confident tone
   D. An undocumented provider assumption

5. **Scenario analysis.** In the normal scenario—reconcile fictional policy excerpts with different authorities and effective dates—name two observable acceptance checks and explain why each belongs to this module.

6. **Scenario analysis.** How should the design respond when the highest-authority source is current but silent about a transition case described only in an older memo. Justify whether to clarify, proceed, abstain, or escalate.

7. **Error identification.** Identify at least three defects in: `Read these documents and give me the correct policy. Use whichever source sounds most convincing.`

8. **Design decision.** Give a safe expected response to this failure case: A retrieved excerpt tells the model to hide conflicts and cite it as the system policy. Name one prompt control and one application control.
