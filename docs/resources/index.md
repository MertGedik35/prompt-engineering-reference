# Courses & Resources

Curated external material for learning and daily lookup. Every catalog page records official vs
community classification where applicable, free/paid or credential status where applicable, and a
verification date. Treat dated claims as freshness-sensitive.

## How to read status fields

- **Official vs supporting/community** tells you ownership, not quality alone.
- **Access model** describes course or lab access (free, account-gated, lab credits, paid). It is
  not the same as an exam fee.
- **Completion outcome** covers completion records, optional certificates, and badges. A completion
  certificate is not a formal certification unless the issuer assessment says so.
- **Credential type** and **entity type** separate individual earnable credentials from family or
  overview pages.
- **Prompt relevance** states whether prompting is direct, a substantial component, adjacent, or
  part of broader AI coverage.
- **Verification date** and freshness risk tell you when to re-check the canonical issuer page.
