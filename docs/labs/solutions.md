# Explained solutions

Explained solutions show reasoning, not only final answers. Open them after an honest exercise and
quiz attempt.

## How to use

1. Attempt the [exercise](exercises.md) and [quiz](quizzes.md) first.
2. Compare your work to the explained solution for the same module.
3. Update your checklist with what you missed.

Authoritative solution pages live in the repository under `labs/solutions/`.
